### Lizard of Oz: Chapter One
A clap of thunder broke the silence. A flash of lightning hit the ground. Smoke filled the air. All around, the animals were very scared.

A fire had started in the bush and the animals needed to find a safe place to hide. Bob Lizard was in a hurry and getting very worried. "Lizzie, Lizzie, where are you? Come out, come out, wherever you are. We have to get to the rocks. The fire is not far," he shouted.

Lizzie didn't answer and was nowhere to be seen. She hadn't heard Bob's loud shouts, so Bob hurried on his way to get to the rock pile. On the way down the hillside, he met other animals preparing to hide.

"Please, Mrs Wombat, have you seen my wife? There's a bush fire coming and I need to know if she's all right," said Bob in a worried voice. Mrs Wombat's house was built in the ground, with a long, narrow burrow and dirt heaped all around.

"Lizzie didn't come this way. In fact, I haven't seen her at all today. But if I do, I'll send her on. Don't worry, she knows where she belongs," said Mrs Wombat.

Bob was moving along quickly. The smell of smoke was getting quite strong now. Then he came upon Mr Snake.

"Please, Mr Snake, can you help me? I'm looking for my wife. I don't want her *getting* *into* *strife*," said Bob.

"Sssssssorry, I haven't ssssssseen Mrs Lizard, but if I do, you'll be the first to know. I'll sssssssend a sssssssignal," Mr Snake hissed.

By now, Bob was running out of time as the bush fire was getting closer. As he got near the pile of rocks he saw Brown Owl, who had been woken up by all the noise and the smell of smoke.

"Brown Owl, Brown Owl, I can't find my Lizzie and I'm beginning to feel dizzy. Can you help me? She's never been away this long. I do hope there's nothing wrong," said Bob.

"Don't worry, my friend, with my big eyes I can see far and wide. I'll look for her as I fly by and 'hoot' if I see her," said the wise Brown Owl.

The wise, old owl flew up into the sky searching for Lizzie as he went. It was hard to see and the smoke was so thick that finding Lizzie might take a magic trick.

### Moving
One evening, Mr Potter came home from work and told his children, David and Natalie, that he had some very special news to tell them. He had an exciting new job. They were pleased about this, until he said that the job was in another city hundreds of kilometres away.

Mrs Potter already knew that they would have to move and buy a new apartment. The children would also have to go to a new school.

Natalie was unhappy because she loved her home, especially her pink fairy bedroom. Her friends next door were upset when she told them they were moving.

David was angry when he realised that he would not be able to play football with his best friend Max after school anymore.

The children's parents suggested that they invite their friends to stay with them in their new apartment during the next school holidays.

Soon it was time to leave their home. The removal truck arrived and some men carried out all the furniture and boxes. They packed them carefully into the huge van.

Mr Potter placed some other bags and boxes which were full of their clothes and the children's special toys into the back of his car. They would unpack them as soon as they arrived at their new apartment.

David and Natalie went to their neighbours and said a teary goodbye to their friends. They promised to phone as soon as they had arrived at their new home.
